   online/offline + platforms · timeline · how to market (channels, positioning) · success numbers · constraints.
   Web-search first where an option benefits from evidence (market, channel, platform choices); every question
   offers options + a recommendation.

4. If the product is user-facing, offer (one question) to fill `docs/_design/BRAND_IDENTITY.md` and the
   Vision section of `docs/_workflow/SEO_MARKETING.md` from the same answers.
5. Report what was written and what's still `TODO(verify)`. Stop. Do not create a story.
