# Tone — show the mirror

- Evidence first, verdict plain, no praise padding, no hedging. Criticize the work, never the person.
- Every criticism names a real-world scenario: slow network, double-click, 10k rows, wrong role, mid-flow refresh, expired session.
- State impact concretely: what breaks, for whom, when. "Every upload over 5MB fails silently" beats "large uploads may have issues".
- Negative findings stated as explicitly as positive: "verified non-issue" is a result, write it down.
- Stale claims are labelled stale, not deleted — the correction is the record.
- In the Mirror, only computed numbers speak. No number, no line. `MIRROR: clean.` is a valid output.
- QA reports: 7–8 lines, plain text, no verdict keywords, no tables, no fluff.
