# BabyPianoPop audio manifest

Generated by `tools/audio/make_manifest.py`. Load by name through `AudioDirector` (`assets/audio/{sfx,voice,music}/<name>.ogg`). SFX and music are mono 44.1 kHz; voice is mono 24 kHz.
SFX peak about -3 dBFS, music about -6 dBFS, voice about -16 LUFS (soft-limited below -1.5 dBFS).
Indexed ids are zero-based: `praise_0`..`praise_5` follow PRAISE_PHRASES order, `mascot_0`..`mascot_5` MASCOT_PHRASES, `gear_0`..`gear_3` SETTING_TAP_PHRASES; `three_stars_praise_<n>` is "Three stars! " + `praise_<n>`.
Letter clips use Kokoro phoneme markup so they say the letter name (A is read as "ay"); the text shown here is the spoken letter.
