# BabyPianoPop audio manifest

Generated by `tools/audio/make_manifest.py` from `eleven_sounds.csv` (prompts) and `eleven_lines.csv` (spoken lines). Load by name through `AudioDirector` (`assets/audio/{sfx,voice,music}/<name>.ogg`). SFX and music are mono 44.1 kHz; voice is mono 24 kHz.
Loudness: one-shots -22 LUFS, `ui_*` -25, music -20, voice -16. The AudioDirector buses balance them: Voice 0 dB (the anchor), SFX -3 dB, Music -6 dB (10 dB under the voice), and a -1 dB limiter on Master.
SFX kept more than once are variant groups: `pop_1`..`pop_4` play as `pop` (`play_sfx(&"pop")` picks one at random, never the same twice in a row).
Indexed voice ids are zero-based: `praise_0`..`praise_11` follow PRAISE_PHRASES order, `mascot_0`..`mascot_5` MASCOT_PHRASES, `gear_0`..`gear_3` SETTING_TAP_PHRASES; `three_stars_praise_<n>` is "Three stars! " + `praise_<n>`.
Spoken text is the line sent to the TTS; some letters are spelled out ("Eff", "Ess") so the voice says the letter's name.
